## A Template for Modern Pizzeria Experience

The published application identifies itself as a **demonstration template** rather than an operating restaurant. The menu dishes, prices, reviews, venue location, and contact data are illustrative prototypes designed to show local business owners what modern web digitization can achieve.

The demo features an allergen-conscious pizza menu, an intuitive cart for takeaway or home delivery, and a visual loyalty card experience.

## Pizza Pass: Digital Loyalty Prototype

Pizza Pass showcases a digital stamp card with progressive reward tracking towards a free pizza, paired with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet visual passes. The page allows visitors to customize their customer name and simulate collecting new digital stamps.

The demo demonstrates the user journey and ergonomics without requiring complex backend billing or third-party merchant accounts.

## Core Interactions

- **Lightweight Digital Menu:** Categorized food listings, clear ingredient breakdowns, and allergen tags.
- **WhatsApp Instant Ordering:** Pickup and home delivery order forms that automatically compose formatted WhatsApp order summaries ready to send.
- **Table QR Code:** Ready-to-scan QR code graphic for placing on dining tables.
- **Location & Google Reviews:** Curated sections showing how a verified Google Maps presence enhances trust.

## Technology & Architecture

The application runs on clean HTML5 and vanilla JavaScript with responsive styling powered by Tailwind CSS. Fast, accessible, and completely free of cumbersome legacy PDF downloads.
