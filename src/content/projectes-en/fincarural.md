## A Digital Experience for Exclusive Rural Tourism

**Mas de l'Alzina** is a web design and engineering showcase created for high-end Mediterranean estates, rural boutique accommodations, and corporate wellness retreat venues in inland Valencia.

The project blends refined art direction — featuring elegant editorial serif typography (*Cormorant Garamond* paired with *Plus Jakarta Sans*) and a warm palette inspired by sunlit lime and stone — with an agile single-page application built on React and TypeScript.

## Core Features

- **Editorial Space Showcase:** High-resolution visual galleries presenting guest suites, courtyards, landscaped gardens, and event spaces.
- **Curated Experience Catalog:** Information on local Mediterranean gastronomy, mindful wellness retreats, and scenic hiking trails.
- **Direct Reservation Request Form:** Clean, accessible inquiry workflow allowing prospective guests and event planners to verify booking dates.
- **Fast Mobile Performance:** Fully responsive layout engineered for rapid mobile loading and comfortable touch navigation.
