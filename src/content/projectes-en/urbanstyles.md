## A Modern E-Commerce Experience for Apparel Brands

**UrbanStyles** is an interactive digital storefront inspired by independent streetwear brands and retro 90s aesthetic. The design emphasizes high-contrast visual identity — deep slate, crisp off-white, and electric yellow accents — paired with a high-performance web architecture built on **Next.js**, **React**, and **TypeScript**.

## Core Features

- **Dynamic Collection Catalog:** Curated capsule releases (*Retro Horizons*, heavyweight 240 GSM tees, hoodies, and lifestyle accessories) with instant category filtering.
- **Interactive Product Customizer:** Size selection (S, M, L, XL), sizing chart modal, and responsive picture tags optimized for mobile viewports.
- **Reactive Shopping Cart:** Client-side cart state management allowing users to update quantities, calculate subtotals, and review orders without page reloads.
- **Speed & Mobile-First UX:** Zero-delay image rendering, font preloading, and touch-first ergonomically positioned controls to maximize conversion rates.
