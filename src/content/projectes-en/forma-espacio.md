## An Exploration of Space & Matter

FORMA / ESPACIO is an architectural visual portfolio centered on spatial research, material honesty, and understated modernism. The site presents concept monographs including *Casa Silencio*, *Materia Viva*, *Umbral*, and *Paisaje Interior*.

## Creative Process

The editorial layout guides visitors through three foundational phases of architectural inquiry: observe, research, and experiment. It explores how built physical space serves as a tangible expression of philosophy.
